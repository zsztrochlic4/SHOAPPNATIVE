Option 1 — delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open StrengthHub Online and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account is disabled immediately and your data is erased by our servers.

Option 2 — request deletion by email

If you can’t sign in, have removed the app, or prefer to request it in writing, email us from the email address on your account:

Email info@strengthhubonline.com

Use the subject “Account deletion request”. To verify it’s you, send it from your account email address; we may ask you to confirm details before we act. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days.

Important — deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. If you subscribe through the app, delete your subscription first: open Settings → Manage or cancel subscription in the app, or cancel through the store you subscribed with (Apple / Google). If you have already deleted your account and can no longer reach the billing portal, email info@strengthhubonline.com and we will cancel it for you. Your payment provider may retain invoice and tax records as required by law.

What we delete

  • Your account and sign-in credentials.
  • Your profile and screening answers (date of birth, sex, height/weight, goals, injuries).
  • Your workout logs and app content.
  • Nutrition estimates you logged (meal photos are not stored on our servers).
  • Any community profile, username and activity records.
  • Your push-notification tokens.

What we may keep, and for how long

  • A minimal deletion record (an account identifier and timestamps only, no personal content) kept as evidence the request was completed.
  • Financial, transaction and tax records that a law requires us or our payment provider (Stripe) to retain.
  • Backups: deletion propagates through our backup cycle within a limited period.
