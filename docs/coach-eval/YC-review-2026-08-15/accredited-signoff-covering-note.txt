Please sign only within your accreditation. If a portion is outside your remit, note that in “Scope reviewed” and it will be routed to the right specialist rather than represented as covered.
Portion

An ESSA-accredited exercise professional (AEP / AES). Confirms scope discipline, injury deferral, and qualitative-only nutrition.
